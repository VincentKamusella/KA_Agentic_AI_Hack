From a sentence to a shortlist

  1. Describe the purchase

    What you need, how much, where it is delivered and, if you like, by when. Add anything else in your own words; it is sorted into must-haves and nice-to-haves that you can review.

  2. Research runs for you

    Several searches look for suppliers, and passages are retrieved from their pages. Each passage is checked against each requirement: pass, fail, or unknown when the page does not say.

  3. Decide with the sources open

    Suppliers that meet every must-have are ranked by your nice-to-haves, up to three on the shortlist. Every result opens the passage and the page it came from.

What you can hold it to

Unknown stays unknown
When a supplier’s pages do not confirm a requirement, it is shown as open and never counted as a pass. You get a draft question to send the supplier yourself.
Must-haves are gates
A supplier that contradicts a must-have is not eligible, whatever else it offers. Nice-to-haves only rank suppliers that passed.
Every result shows its evidence
Each rating links to the passage and the source page behind it, and says whether a model or a fixed rule produced it.
You make the decision
QuotePilot places no orders and sends no messages. It does not guarantee prices, capacity or delivery dates; those are for you to confirm with the supplier.
