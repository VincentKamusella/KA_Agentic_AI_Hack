Find suppliers

1. Discover suppliers and retrieve evidence

Multiple searches explore manufacturers, specialists, directories, and distributors across your chosen regions and languages. We retrieve relevant pages and PDFs from candidate sites and retain source passages. Search summaries are used only to find candidates. Blocked or unreadable pages remain research gaps, not supplier failures. Coverage shows the searches performed and why research stopped; it does not establish completeness. Company metadata and observed redirects help identify duplicates, but identity and terms still need human verification.

2. Assess every criterion

Each source excerpt is assessed as pass, fail, or unknown. Live decisions below the provisional 85% confidence threshold abstain. Conflicting evidence also becomes unknown. The threshold has not been calibrated on customer examples.

3. Apply mandatory gates

A mandatory failure makes a supplier ineligible. An unknown mandatory criterion requires verification. Preference scores cannot override either outcome. With no mandatory criteria, the shortlist reflects preferences only.

4. Rank eligible suppliers

Supported preferences earn 100 points; failed or unknown preferences earn zero. Each criterion uses your chosen weight, and all preference weights stay in the denominator. Ties are broken by evidence coverage, then supplier ID. Up to three eligible suppliers are shortlisted.

5. Verify before purchasing

Quantity and destination guide discovery unless you include them as explicit criteria. This is a research shortlist, not a quotation or purchasing approval. QuotePilot does not place orders or send supplier messages.
